# Independent-scenario pilot protocol

Status: prepared; no external scenarios have been collected or evaluated.

The target pilot contains 20–30 cases written by a qualified contributor who has not seen LGR outputs. Cases should cover at least two documented policy/workflow families and include permitted actions, explicit denials, missing witnesses, incompatible top-priority evidence, and unresolved conflicts.

For each case, the contributor receives source policy/workflow documents and the blank template in this directory. They record the source passages, graph facts, observation time, scope, expected evidence, expected action decision, and rationale before the case is executed. LGR output is revealed only after the set is frozen.

The research record must identify:

- the contributor's role and relevant qualifications, with consent;
- whether the case is adapted from a real system, public source, or constructed example;
- every synthetic ontology mapping, trust order, registry entry, policy rule, and workflow transition;
- disagreements and their adjudication without deleting difficult cases;
- permissions for restricted data and any required human-participant review.

A public temporal knowledge graph may support a temporal-retrieval test. Governance tables added by the researcher remain synthetic and must be reported separately. AI-generated scenarios are additional generated cases, not independent human validation.

Primary outputs are exact evidence-set agreement, action-set agreement, invalid admissions, false denials, and reason-code agreement with explicit numerators and denominators. The manuscript may claim feasibility only for the collected cases.
